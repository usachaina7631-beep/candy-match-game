❓ How to Play

🍬 Ek candy ko tap karo, phir uske bagal wali candy ko tap karo — ya swipe/drag karo — dono swap ho jayengi.

🎯 Ek line me 3 ya zyada same candies match karo to wo pop hongi aur score milega. Level ka score target moves khatam hone se pehle pura karo.

💥 Match 4 = Striped Candy (safed dhariyan — poori row/column saaf)
🎁 L/T shape match = Wrapped Candy (sunehra ✦ nishan — 3×3 blast)
🌈 Match 5 = Colour Bomb (ghumti hui rainbow goli — ek poora colour saaf)
