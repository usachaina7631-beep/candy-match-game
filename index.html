❓ How to Play

🍬 Ek candy ko tap karo, phir uske bagal wali candy ko tap karo — ya swipe/drag karo — dono swap ho jayengi.

🎯 Ek line me 3 ya zyada same candies match karo to wo pop hongi aur score milega. Level ka score target moves khatam hone se pehle pura karo.

💥 Match 4 = Striped Candy (safed dhariyan — poori row/column saaf)
🎁 L/T shape match = Wrapped Candy (sunehra ✦ nishan — 3×3 blast)
🌈 Match 5 = Colour Bomb (ghumti hui rainbow goli — ek poora colour saaf)

🗺️ Worlds & Sitare: 300 levels 30 worlds me bante hain. Har level par 1–3 ⭐ kamao — map (🗺️ Levels) par har level ke sitare dikhte hain.

❤️ Lives: kul 5 lives. Level haarne par 1 life kati hai; har 30 minute me 1 life apne aap wapas bhar jaati hai.

🟩 Jelly levels: kuch levels (har 7va) par board ke khanon par hari jelly jami hoti hai — us khane par match karo to jelly saaf hoti hai. Saari jelly saaf = jeet!

⌨️ Keyboard: Arrows = cursor, Enter/Space = select/swap, WASD = swap, H = hint, P = pause.
